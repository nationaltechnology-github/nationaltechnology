Our History

Three Decades.
Three Technology Eras.

Origin
Founded in 1997, National Technology began as a specialist in the high-stakes world of HP 3000 and HP 1000 systems, providing the mission-critical hardware and architectural support that powered national enterprise back-offices and real-time process control environments. As the technological landscape transitioned from physical mainframes to distributed systems, our focus evolved toward strategic technical consulting. Today, we carry that legacy of systemic integrity into the era of AI-driven enterprise systems — providing the strategic technology leadership that critical industries demand.
Timeline

Statement of Evolution

1997 — 2005

HP 3000 Hardware & Systemic Support

Mission-critical mainframe architecture, hardware provisioning, and enterprise back-office systems for national-scale operations on HP MPE platforms.

2005 — 2020

Strategic Enterprise Consulting & System Modernization

Guiding enterprises through the mainframe-to-distributed transition. Infrastructure strategy, data migration, and technical leadership for organizations navigating platform shifts.

2021 — Present

AI Integration Advisory & Strategic Technology Leadership

Strategic technology leadership delivering AI integration advisory, legacy data modernization, and enterprise architecture consulting for domains where accuracy is non-negotiable.

Operating History

Client Environments

National Technology provided enterprise technology services — from HP 3000 and HP 1000 hardware and systems architecture to full-scope outsourced IT operations — across a range of high-reliability environments. Client confidentiality prevents us from naming organizations directly. The following reflects the scope and character of our operational history.

Insurance Carriers

Full-scope outsourced IT operations with dedicated on-site technical staff. Voice systems integration, document scanning and indexing platforms, claims infrastructure, and day-to-day technology management for a regulated carrier environment.

Radiopharmaceutical Manufacturing & Distribution

Mission-critical systems supporting the production and logistics of radioactive medical materials. Regulated environments with zero tolerance for downtime.

Municipal Law Enforcement

Enterprise systems for police departments — records management, dispatch infrastructure, and operational data platforms requiring around-the-clock reliability.

Higher Education

Administrative and academic computing infrastructure for universities. Student records, enrollment systems, and institutional data on HP 3000 platforms.

Naval Weapons Systems Maintenance

HP 1000 and HP 3000 hardware upgrades at U.S. Navy ordnance and weapons systems maintenance facilities. Real-time process control and enterprise infrastructure in secure, classified environments.

Performing Arts Institutions

Enterprise back-office systems for major cultural organizations — ticketing, donor management, and operational infrastructure at institutional scale.

Pension & Benefits Administration

Fiduciary data systems and benefits processing for large-scale pension plans. Regulatory compliance, financial reporting, and high-volume participant records.
