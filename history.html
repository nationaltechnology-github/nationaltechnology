Our History

Three Decades.
Three Technology Eras.

Origin
Founded in 1997, National Technology began as a specialist in the high-stakes world of HP 3000 and HP 1000 systems, providing the mission-critical hardware and architectural support that powered national enterprise back-offices and real-time process control environments. As the technological landscape transitioned from physical mainframes to distributed systems, our focus evolved toward strategic technical consulting. Today, we carry that legacy of systemic integrity into the era of precision AI systems — designing the retrieval, verification, and evidence-grounding architectures that critical industries demand.
Timeline

Statement of Evolution

1997 — 2005

HP 3000 Hardware & Systemic Support

Mission-critical mainframe architecture, hardware provisioning, and enterprise back-office systems for national-scale operations on HP MPE platforms.

2005 — 2020

Strategic Enterprise Consulting & System Modernization

Guiding enterprises through the mainframe-to-distributed transition. Infrastructure strategy, data migration, and technical leadership for organizations navigating platform shifts.

2021 — Present

Precision AI Systems & Retrieval Architecture

Fractional CTO engagements delivering multi-stage retrieval and verification pipeline design, legacy data modernization, and strategic technical leadership for domains where accuracy is non-negotiable.
