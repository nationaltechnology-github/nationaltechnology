Privacy Policy

Effective March 28, 2023

Overview

National Technology ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This policy describes what information we collect, how we use it, and how it is protected. We do not sell, rent, or share information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to operate our business and respond to inquiries:

  • Contact information you provide directly — name, email address, phone number, and any details included in your message when you contact us through our website, by phone, or by email.
  • Voicemail recordings and caller phone numbers when you call our business line and leave a message. Caller phone numbers are captured automatically by the telephone network.
  • Standard server logs generated when you visit our website, including IP address, browser type, and pages viewed. These are used for security and operational purposes only.

How We Use Information

Information we collect is used solely to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and conduct business communications.
  • Send internal operational alerts to the business owner — for example, an SMS notification to the owner's own mobile phone when a voicemail is left on the company business line.
  • Maintain the security and integrity of our systems.

SMS and Messaging

Our SMS messaging is used internally only. Automated text messages are sent exclusively to the business owner's own mobile phone number, which the owner has configured as the alert recipient. We do not send marketing, promotional, or unsolicited SMS messages to customers, prospects, or any third parties. Mobile phone numbers and message content related to internal alerts are never shared with third parties.

Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes. Information is disclosed only when:

  • Required by law, subpoena, or valid legal process.
  • Necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of National Technology or others.
  • Provided to service providers (such as telephony and email infrastructure vendors) acting on our behalf, who are bound by confidentiality obligations and are permitted to use the information only to deliver the contracted service.

Data Retention

We retain information only as long as needed for the purposes described above or as required by law. Voicemail recordings and call records may be retained for business and reference purposes and are deleted when no longer needed.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No method of electronic transmission or storage is fully secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

You may contact us at any time to ask what information we hold about you, request correction of inaccurate information, or request deletion of your information, subject to legal and operational requirements.

Children's Privacy

Our services are directed to businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page indicates when it was last revised. Continued use of our services after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be directed to National Technology at +1 (301) 450-8000 or through the contact page.
